Uses the PowerLab 8 serial protocol published by Revolectrix (Ram status packet, SelP, SelC/D/M/Y, SelE) at 19200 baud, through the ESP32 PL8-Bridge (Bluetooth LE), a Bluetooth serial adapter such as an HC-05, or a FUIM3 on USB. Safety screens still have to be acknowledged, and the charger enforces its own limits whatever this page sends. Works in Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS.
